Ekko: find work you repeat

With your consent, a small recorder on your own computer notices which work apps you switch between. Second Shift then looks for work you repeat, so you can decide whether it is worth automating.

What Ekko records, and what it never records

It records the name of the app in front on your computer (for example “Microsoft Excel”), when you switched to it and for how long. Only the apps you choose are noticed.

It never records window titles, what is on your screen, what you type, the clipboard, web addresses, file names, or anything inside a file, email or message.

Nothing is recorded until you press Start. Recording stops when you pause, stop or withdraw your consent.

What is noticed is sent to your organisation’s Second Shift server and stored there encrypted. Only you can open it: Second Shift has no view across people. Each activity is deleted after {{ ekko.retention_days }} days, and you can erase everything sooner.
